# CNT-02 Consumer Survey — India

**Purpose:** Academic research on online electronics and appliances shopping behaviour in India.

**Consent:** Participation is voluntary. Responses will be analysed for academic purposes in aggregate. Do not enter your name, phone number, exact address, payment information or other direct identifier. You may skip a question or stop at any time. Proceed only if you consent.

## Questions

1. Do you consent to participate? Yes / No (terminate if No).
2. State/UT (select one; do not collect street address).
3. Residence: Urban / Rural.
4. Age band: Under 18 / 18–24 / 25–34 / 35–44 / 45–54 / 55+.
5. Have you purchased any product online in the last 12 months? Yes / No.
6. Which electronics category do you most prefer to buy online? Smartphone / Laptop or computer / TV / Large appliance / Small appliance / Accessories / Other / Not applicable.
7. How many electronics or appliance purchases have you made online in the last 12 months? 0 / 1 / 2–3 / 4–5 / 6+.
8. What is your typical spend per online electronics purchase? Below ₹1,000 / ₹1,000–₹4,999 / ₹5,000–₹14,999 / ₹15,000–₹29,999 / ₹30,000–₹59,999 / ₹60,000+.
9. How sensitive are you to price when buying electronics online? Not at all / Slightly / Moderately / Very / Extremely.
10. How important are discounts or coupons? 1 (not important) to 5 (very important).
11. Would no-cost EMI or instalments influence your choice? Not at all / A little / Somewhat / A lot / Not eligible or unsure.
12. Would an exchange offer influence your choice? Not at all / A little / Somewhat / A lot / Not applicable.
13. How much does delivery cost influence your decision? Not at all / A little / Somewhat / A lot.
14. What maximum price increase (all else unchanged) would make you postpone or switch a planned electronics purchase? 0% / 1–5% / 6–10% / 11–15% / More than 15% / Unsure.

## Sampling and reporting guidance

Aim for variation across Indian regions, states, urban/rural residence and age groups. Record recruitment method and field dates. A convenience sample is exploratory, not nationally representative. Report valid response count, missing data, sample composition and limitations. Do not apply national population weights without a defensible weighting frame and method.

## Coding notes

Use response categories consistently with data/survey_template.csv. Store age, spending and frequency as categorical bands unless reliable numeric values are collected. Keep identifiers anonymous and separate consent records from responses. Do not claim causal price elasticity from stated-preference answers.
